2Daily routine

Your daily routine drives both the lighting plan and the thermostat setback periods.

Weekends are treated as at-home days without a work block. Sunrise and sunset per season follow the Buffalo approximations used by the lighting planner; actual indoor daylight depends on windows, orientation, shading and weather.

3Electricity tariff

This one price schedule is applied to every hour of heating, cooling, lighting and appliances.

National Grid published VTOU delivery rates: 1.299¢/kWh off-peak (11 PM–7 AM); 12.633¢/kWh on-peak (7 AM–11 PM). Super-peak commodity period: summer weekdays 2–6 PM (June–August). Supply fields are user-editable examples, not live prices. Excludes riders, taxes and account eligibility.

Each appliance keeps its energy use, but its busiest hour is moved into the window you allow, at the cheapest time on your tariff. The dryer follows the washer. The shifted schedules run inside EnergyPlus, so their electricity is part of the same bill as heating, cooling and lighting.
